# Add action risk badges

## Goal

Show a compact Low/Med/High risk badge beside Git Next actions with a one-line reason.

## Do

- Derive risk level from current repository state and action type.
- Show a short reason such as dirty tree, diverged history, or history rewrite.
- Keep the badge visible in action entry points without opening Doctor first.

## Keep

- Badges are advisory; they do not replace confirmation flows.
- Reuse Safe Guard signals instead of new Git probes where possible.
- Avoid alarm fatigue; reserve High for genuinely destructive or hard-to-undo actions.

## Done

- Supported actions show risk badges with concise reasons.
- Risk levels stay consistent with Safe Guard outcomes.
- Focused tests and build pass.

## Result

Added state-derived risk levels and concise hover reasons to Pull/Push actions in Graph and Source Control. Dirty state, tracking divergence, missing Upstream, active operations, and destructive operation types use the same Safe Guard signals; badges are advisory and leave confirmation flows intact. Verified 18 focused tests, build, and diff check.
